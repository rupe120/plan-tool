- MEDIUM `[disposition-without-refusal]` [confidence: limited]: Step 6 says approval "records reviewer dispositions for semantic findings with no refusal keyed on semantic content". Its intent line says "each uncertain finding ends in an explicit reviewer disposition". Architecture says the semantic class "requires a reviewer disposition" but "never blocks". The plan does not say what happens when an approver supplies no disposition for an uncertain finding. Two readers would build different things:
  - approval proceeds and the finding is recorded as undisposed;
  - approval is refused until a disposition is given, which is a refusal keyed on semantic content and breaks the Not-allowed list.

  Steps 1 (disposition vocabulary and exit path), 6 and 7 ("accept reviewer dispositions on approval") each leave this open. The plan also does not say where a disposition is stored, given that step 4's evidence record is create-only and stamp-bound.
  - Fix: State that a missing disposition never refuses approval. Say that it is recorded as an explicit "undisposed" value in the step 1 vocabulary, and name the record that holds dispositions, so the "explicit disposition" outcome and the "no semantic refusal" fence can both be true. Reconcile steps 1, 6 and 7 to this.
- MEDIUM `[removed-id-source]` [confidence: limited]: Step 2 reads removed ids from `artifact.removals`, and Architecture says "Removed ids are the `removed_id` records on `artifact.removals`, read through the predecessor chain". Two code facts at HEAD leave the source unclear:
  - `plan_tool/roadmap_candidate.py:_assemble_candidate_artifacts` runs `replace(after, feedback=(), removals=())` on the `feedback_free` path. A candidate artifact on that path carries no removals.
  - `ROADMAP_FORMAT.md` § "The removal record" says "Pruning a carried record is the reviewer's move, and it is how a removed row is later allowed back". A pruned record drops out of the check on purpose.

  The plan does not say whether the check reads the candidate's own artifact, the predecessor's artifact, or the union. It also does not say whether a `feedback_free` candidate is checked against its predecessor's removals. Implementers could ship checks that disagree on which ids are "removed". This matters on the Integration case, where IPPT-19's own symptom depends on a removal that the lineage still carries.
  - Fix: Name the source in step 1/step 2. State that the check reads the removals carried by the candidate's predecessor artifact plus any the candidate adds. State that a pruned record is deliberately no longer checked, and say how a `feedback_free` candidate is handled.
- MEDIUM `[semantic-review-unavailable]` [confidence: limited]: Step 5 adds a bounded model call "under a fixed attempt budget". Step 6 regenerates missing or stale evidence "at review time", and says "never a refusal". The plan names no outcome for the semantic call failing, running out of its attempt budget, or returning unparseable output while approval is under way. A reader could build any of these:
  - a refusal, which breaks "never a refusal";
  - a silently empty semantic section, which looks like "no findings";
  - a labeled "semantic review unavailable" state.

  The third is the only one that keeps the reviewer informed. The plan has no criterion that distinguishes "reviewed, nothing found" from "not reviewed".
  - Fix: Add a closed evidence state for a semantic review that did not complete. Require the surfaces in step 7 to label it, require that it never blocks approval, and add a Testing case and acceptance criterion for it.
