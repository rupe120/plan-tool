- MEDIUM `[stack-no-removal-enforcement]` [confidence: limited]: The plan commits to "Stack rows can be added but not removed, as with hosting rows, so a candidate can never turn a recorded stack back into 'stack not recorded'". Totality only binds when the collection is present, so a candidate that deletes every stack row and also drops the `stack` key leaves an artifact with no collection. That artifact passes every gate, because absence never binds. The guarantee therefore depends entirely on step 3's stage-row policy refusing removal of the collection and of its rows. Step 3's summary describes the policy as "concern field fixed and rows addable" and says nothing about removal. The Testing bullet "a candidate cannot remove a stack row or the collection" has no step that owns it. Name step 3 (`feedback targets and revisable through candidates`) as the owner of the removal refusal, and name the layer that refuses removing the whole collection. A per-row policy cannot express that case, since the key is a collection-level change. Also have the step 3 tests assert it.
  - Fix: Make step 3 the explicit owner of "stack rows and the collection are not removable". Say which layer refuses a candidate that drops the `stack` key, and add the test to step 3 and to the Testing section.
