  - Fix: revise step 1 (and Architecture steps 3/5, Testing criterion 1, Acceptance criterion 1, and References, which all currently assume read-only reuse of existing records) to add persistence of the per-finding audit-step target to the saved review schema — a new field on `Recommendation`/`RunFinding`, threaded through `save_review` and recorded in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` — since no existing persisted field carries it.
- HIGH `[consensus-drop-vs-material-set]`: Architecture step 4 says a finding that can't resolve a consensus target should be "dropped... from consensus for the round pair," but step 5 says to feed the newest round's material finding set into `evaluate_arm_clustering` "unchanged." `evaluate_arm_clustering` has no per-finding drop path: for every id in `material_finding_ids` it looks up `consensus_targets.get(finding_id)` and returns `None` for the whole advisory the moment one id's target is missing (`plan_tool/audit_shapes.py:evaluate_arm_clustering`). A finding that just appeared in the newest round has one sample in the window and fails step 2's "≥2 passes" rule, which will happen on most rounds, not rarely — so read literally, step 5 lets one fresh finding silence the entire cross-round advisory almost every time it would otherwise fire.
  - Fix: specify in step 1 that the material-finding set actually passed to `evaluate_arm_clustering` is the subset that resolved a cross-round consensus target, not the newest round's full material set, and update step 5's "unchanged" language to say so.
- HIGH `[cross-round-window-size]`: Architecture step 1 says to mirror `classify_impact_recurrence` / `classify_edit_drift`'s windowing, which defaults to `WINDOW_SIZE = 3` and returns nothing at all unless that many same-stage passes exist (`_impact_recurrence_trailing_window`'s `len(compatible) < window` check), and `classify_impact_recurrence` further requires a candidate to appear in every pass of the window, not just some of them. Step 2 instead says a finding only needs a match in "≥2 passes... mirroring the existing `len(sample_ids) < 2` guard," and Testing criterion 5 expects the advisory to fire with "`--audit-passes 1` with ≥2 qualifying prior rounds." These don't pin down the same design: reusing `WINDOW_SIZE=3` as written blocks any advisory until 3 rounds exist, which contradicts the 2-round testing criterion, and it's unstated whether a finding needs to match in all of the window's passes or just 2 of them.
  - Fix: pin one window size and per-finding match-count rule for the new consensus builder (state whether it reuses `WINDOW_SIZE=3` with a 3-round floor or a smaller window sized to fire at 2 rounds, and whether a finding must match every window pass or just 2), and align Testing criterion 5 to whichever is chosen.
