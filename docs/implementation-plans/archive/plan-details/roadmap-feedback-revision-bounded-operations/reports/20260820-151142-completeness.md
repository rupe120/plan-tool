  - Fix: Define one explicit outcome for reconciliation that leaves an open feedback target unresolved, then align the no-refusal promise, response authority, tests, and acceptance criteria to it.
- HIGH `[canonical-noop-upstream]`: Step 4's Architecture says the “canonical parsed form becomes the working artifact… never the raw on-disk bytes,” but its feedback-free rule says to append the “preflight-loaded predecessor” to `loaded`. Testing and the feedback-free acceptance criterion repeat that carrier rule; at HEAD, `plan_tool/roadmap.py:_load_stage_artifact` stores raw disk text in `_LoadedArtifact.text`, and `_render_stage_prompt` forwards `loaded.text`, so a later selected stage can receive unreprocessed content without a write or reload.
  - Fix: Define the feedback-free `loaded` entry after reconciliation as an in-memory canonical artifact and require later prompt/context construction to use it rather than raw predecessor text.
- HIGH `[hosting-add-row]`: Step 1's authority matrix admits `hosting` for `add-row`, and Testing requires an addition for every admitted collection, while Out of scope forbids changes to what existing stage gates check. `plan_tool/roadmap_gates:check_hosting_totality` requires every valid predecessor to have exactly one `compute`, `model`, and `topology` row, so an added known facet duplicates one and an unknown facet fails; `ROADMAP_FORMAT.md` also defines hosting as identity-only rather than a prose or revision target.
  - Fix: Remove `hosting` from the stage-envelope vocabulary, or explicitly re-scope a closed hosting replacement contract that preserves its fixed-facet and identity-only rules.
- HIGH `[local-reconciliation-route]`: `--revise-feedback` is promised in the Architecture to “normalize and log non-feedback hand edits, then continue against the same canonical working artifact,” but step 4 does not include its actual entry point, `roadmap_revision_run:run_local_feedback_revision`, or `tests/test_roadmap_revision_run.py`. At HEAD that route passes raw `artifact_text` onward and `roadmap_revision:preflight_feedback_revision` rejects a render mismatch, so the local profile has no planned route for the required normalization log and continuation.
  - Fix: Give step 4 an explicit local orchestration, run-log, and test route that carries the normalized predecessor through selected-row validation, merge, and publication.
- HIGH `[hand-edit-seed-freshness]`: A feedback-free re-entry can accept a hand-edited `PlansArtifact` as its canonical working artifact while the Architecture, Testing, and acceptance criteria require no seed-state change unless a bounded merge succeeds. `roadmap_seed_state` records only seed status and digest, and `roadmap.py:_invalidate_roadmap_seeds` runs after writes; a green no-op after a manual plan change therefore leaves derived seeds marked current even though their source changed.
  - Fix: Make a reprocessed plans artifact fail closed by invalidating derived seeds before its no-op or later bounded attempt, and limit the zero-seed-change rule to predecessors that needed no reprocessing.
