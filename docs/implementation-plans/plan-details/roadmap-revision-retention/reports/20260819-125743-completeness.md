  - Fix: note in Architecture (or as an acceptance criterion) that the reused rejection needs a field-tampering-specific remediation clause distinct from the "drop the record" text meant for a genuinely fabricated removal.

  - Fix: broaden step 6 to rename every `MODE_REVIEW`/`--mode review` reference across `roadmap_view.py`'s code and docstrings, `cli/roadmap_cmd.py`'s help text, and the three hand-maintained docs (README.md, docs/ROADMAP_WALKTHROUGH.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) — the generated `docs/cli/roadmap.md` follows automatically on regeneration and needs no separate edit.
- MEDIUM `[closed-row-deletion-scope]`: The Overview states closed feedback rows "are retained in the `feedback` collection (never deleted)," but step 3's title commits to "keep the reviewer's delete-the-row close working" — the very mechanism the Problem statement blames for today's loss ("A reviewer resolves a row only by deleting it before a run"). Every check in this design (`check_feedback_integrity`; the `check_design_row_continuity` docstring: "Feedback is transient (the reviewer closes a row by deleting it)") only ever judges the model's output against the reviewer-edited predecessor, so nothing stops a reviewer from deleting an already-`closed` row by hand — "never deleted" holds only against the model, never against the reviewer. A reader can take "never deleted" as an artifact-wide guarantee the tool enforces, or (the reading step 3 actually implies) as a promise scoped to the model's own edits while the reviewer keeps full deletion rights over any row, closed included.
  - Fix: state in Architecture or Overview that retention binds only the model's own edits, not the reviewer, so the docs step doesn't ship "never deleted" as an unqualified claim.

Step 3 adds `FeedbackState.CLOSED` and step 7 documents it, but neither step names these two extra sites — only the narrative was flagged before.
  - Fix: when step 7 documents `closed`, update all three ROADMAP_FORMAT.md sites together — the schema block's `state` comment, the Closed vocabularies table's "Three members, and no closed" line, and the Five situations narrative — not just the one already found.
- HIGH `[removal-snapshot-authorship]`: Step 1 adds a snapshot holding "the removed row's rendered content as it stood at removal," but nothing says who writes that text — the model, describing in its own words the row it is dropping, or the roadmap orchestrator, mechanically copying the row's own fields before the drop.   - Fix: state in step 1 (or ## Architecture) whether the snapshot is model-authored or mechanically captured from the predecessor row, and if model-authored, have the revision prompt instruct the model to reproduce the row's own rendered fields rather than paraphrase, even without a verifying gate.
