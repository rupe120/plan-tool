- HIGH `[recorded-field-research-scope]`: Step 2 (`Load roadmap product names, field origins, and descriptions into research…`) leaves the research input rule contradictory and unbound. Overview says `_build_research_context` “also reads” product strings “not just the plan title and step titles,” while Architecture’s Scope of the read says “Only technologies named in a **recorded field** are researched and gated” and prose-only technology “is out”; `_prepare_create_plan` currently sends `body_source` directly to research for `--from` and `--from-existing`, using `_build_research_context` only without a body. The plan also never says which accepted roadmap belongs to a given create invocation, so an implementation can still mine prose, choose an unrelated roadmap, or skip roadmap inputs.
  - Fix: Make the recorded-field scope authoritative by binding a roadmap-derived create to one accepted roadmap, routing every research entry through only its recorded product fields, and retaining current behavior when no roadmap is bound.
- HIGH `[per-product-source-verdict]`: Overview requires results “per technology rather than for the batch,” Architecture makes “the research step returned at least one source” the binary gate fact, step 2 carries field origins, and step 3 refuses “each roadmap product”; none defines the required per-product result. At HEAD, `_REFERENCE_RESPONSE_CONTRACT`, `propose_references_from_claude`, and `Reference` produce one untagged `list[Reference]`, so a source for product A gives no verdict for product B; `ROADMAP_FORMAT.md` and `_parse_hosting_rows` / `_parse_store_rows` also impose no uniqueness on optional product names, so one failed name can have several originating fields despite the Acceptance criterion promising “the field that named it.”
  - Fix: Define a per-product source/no-source verdict that retains every originating field, chooses repeated-product handling, and supplies the refusal message before References publication.
- HIGH `[research-refusal-phase]`: Step 3 says refusal is “before publication” and must bypass “References replacement,” but `create._prepare_create_plan` researches before `_publish_create_plan` while `_converge_create_plan` invokes `propose_references_from_claude` again after publication and can call `_patch_plan_body_section`. The plan never chooses whether a later no-source result reopens refusal, preserves the previously accepted References, or skips replacement, so `--iterate` can produce materially different outcomes and may fail after the plan already exists.
  - Fix: Define the gate’s phase boundary and the required no-source behavior for every later reference refresh, including whether accepted References remain unchanged.
- MEDIUM `[refusal-retry-recovery]`: Architecture promises not to discard completed work, Risks says an operator who adds a description should not pay for full re-proposal, and steps 2 and 3 promise checkpoint preservation; however no retry contract says which proposed work remains reusable and which research result is rechecked after the recorded row changes. `_initial_steps_input_hash` and `_references_input_hash` currently have separate inputs, so either stale empty research can survive or earlier work can be regenerated unnecessarily.
  - Fix: Specify and test the refusal-retry boundary: a row edit rechecks the affected research while retaining pre-refusal proposal work and leaving publication state untouched.
- HIGH `[operator-description-round-trip]`: Step 1 adds operator descriptions, step 2 reads them, and Acceptance criteria documents them on both row kinds, but `roadmap_revision.HostingRowPayload` and `StoreRowPayload` are closed models with only `product` and their `to_row()` methods cannot carry a description into `roadmap_artifacts.HostingRow` or `StoreRow`. A roadmap revision cannot add or preserve the fallback description once an unrelated row edit occurs.
  - Fix: Extend step 1’s row-field contract through the roadmap revision input/output path and test that descriptions survive unrelated hosting and store edits.
- HIGH `[roadmap-source-selection]`: Step 2 (`Load roadmap product names, field origins, and descriptions into research…`) calls the source “the accepted roadmap” without defining how a `create` invocation selects it or handles a missing or unreadable association. `create._prepare_create_plan` sends `body_source` for `--from-existing` and calls `_build_research_context` only without `--from`, while `roadmap_seed_state.locate_tracked_seed` is used only by `cli.authoring._confirm_invalidated_roadmap_seed`; the described route therefore cannot reach the associated roadmap during the normal seed-backed create path.
  - Fix: define how a tracked seed resolves its current roadmap, how that input reaches the actual research call, and that unassociated or unavailable roadmap input follows the unchanged path.
