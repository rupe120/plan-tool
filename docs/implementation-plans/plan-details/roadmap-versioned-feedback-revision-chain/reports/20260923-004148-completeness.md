Architecture and Scope guardrails require every candidate to bind one “direct source,” but Architecture also says a replacement retains the reviewed candidate’s root, source, and batch evidence; an implementer can either set the replacement source to its immediate reviewed predecessor or preserve the original batch source.
```text
Architecture: “Every candidate records both `root_version_id` and `source_revision_id`; validation walks source ancestry back to the declared root…”

Scope guardrails: “One candidate must bind one accepted root, one direct source, and one immutable ledger snapshot.”
```
  - Fix: Define the replacement’s immediate predecessor, direct source, and retained original-batch source as distinct or identical bindings, then align review, ancestry validation, and promotion rules to that choice.
