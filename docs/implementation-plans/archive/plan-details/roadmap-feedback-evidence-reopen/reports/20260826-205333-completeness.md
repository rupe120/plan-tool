- HIGH `[ordinary-already-responded-protection]`: Ledgered manual clear becomes an ordinary re-escalation bypass: Architecture, Risks, Testing, and acceptance criteria 1 and 9 permit `awaiting-review` → `open` with no active response or pointer; step 1 (`Define active response-ledger pointers, operator attestations, and per-attempt identities`) and its clear test preserve that shape; and at HEAD `roadmap_escalation_selection.py:select_receipt_feedback()` and `roadmap_revision.py:preflight_feedback_revision()` accept it. Architecture and step 1 also treat absent ledger fields as legacy, while step 3 (`Admit one verified or explicit operator-attested reopen, preserve the old response, and restore open eligibility`) admits legacy responses, so stripping metadata creates the same untracked path; either route bypasses evidence invalidation and can issue new evidence without a fresh reissue identity.
  - Fix: distinguish genuine pre-ledger and manually cleared history from eligible open feedback, and make every ordinary receipt and revision admission refuse those states until the bounded reopen records a fresh attempt.
- HIGH `[reopen-audit-history]`: Verified reopen history has no persisted redacted reason: Architecture and acceptance criterion 3 allow a verified response to reopen “without a second attestation,” while step 1 gives an invalidation reason only to `operator-attested` bindings and step 4 (`Atomically publish the open-state reopen, bind its original log, and recover safely`) stores only original provenance plus a completion binding. Step 5 (`Add exclusive retirement-record, reopen, and recovery CLI modes with a safe review view`) requires its parsed-state-only review view to “display provenance class, invalidation reason, retirement-record or evidence identity,” so a completed verified `retired` or `receipt-invalid` reopen cannot supply the promised later audit view without reading forbidden evidence or logs.
  - Fix: persist one immutable redacted reopen record for every admitted route, preserving the original verified binding while recording the classifier result and safe evidence or retirement identity for CLI and review use.
- MEDIUM `[recovered-run-end-semantics]`: Step 4 says recovery appends a `RunEnd` carrying the original run ID and completion-event ID, but at HEAD `plan_tool/run_log.py:RunEnd` and `RunLogWriter.run_end()` also emit outcome, exit code, and duration. Its completion binding stores only attempt, selector, run ID, path, and completion ID, so a failed append leaves recovery with no defined terminal facts; different implementations can record success, pending, or zero-duration completion for the same run.
  - Fix: define one deterministic complete terminal-event policy for recovered reopen logs and bind or derive every recovered terminal field before strict validation and append.
- HIGH `[response-ledger-immutability]`: Step 1 calls the response ledger append-only and immutable in Architecture, Testing, and acceptance criterion 1, but its implementation and tests omit the predecessor-aware protection that enforces that promise. At HEAD `plan_tool/roadmap_gates.py:check_feedback_integrity()` compares carried state, request, and response text only, so a generated artifact can remove a ledger, pointer, or active attempt and turn a ledgered row back into a legacy-shaped row without failing the existing gate.
  - Fix: define and test a predecessor-aware history rule that permits only the named response-writer and guarded-reopen changes while preserving prior ledger, pointer, and attempt facts everywhere else.
- HIGH `[retired-evidence-admission-path]`: The `retired` route cannot admit the Problem statement’s archived version-1 evidence: Architecture and step 2 accept retained bytes only below `.plan-tool/evidence/history/retirements/`, but `BUG_BACKLOG.md` records the relevant Integration evidence under `.plan-tool/evidence/history/2026-08-26-v1-retirement-archive/`; step 2 and step 5 provide no supported transfer or registration path between them. The record producer therefore refuses the concrete archived evidence needed to reopen the stated row.
  - Fix: define a supported immutable admission or migration path from the established archive layout into the fixed retirement-record boundary, and test it against the stated archived-evidence case.
