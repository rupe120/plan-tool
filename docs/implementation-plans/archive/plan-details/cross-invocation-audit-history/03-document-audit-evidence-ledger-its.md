## Why

`plan_tool/convergence_diagnosis.py:load_report_history` deliberately admits only eligible final sidecars to the convergence diagnosis, while step 2 makes incomplete and unreadable completeness artifacts visible beside that diagnosis. Without clear documentation, an operator can mistake a visible checkpoint, report-only file, or orphan sidecar for trajectory evidence, or read `no-history` as proof that no saved artifact exists. This step makes the two meanings clear at the user-guide and architecture levels.

## Implementation

- **Foundational logic:** Document saved-artifact visibility as separate from a convergence decision. The ledger tells an operator what is on disk; `plan_tool/convergence_diagnosis.py:diagnose_convergence` still decides only from eligible history.

- **Use the landed vocabulary:** In `docs/how-to/pipeline/audit.md`, give one operator-facing home to the empty `no-saved-audits` result and every ledger state step 2 ships: `report-only`, `orphan-sidecar`, `malformed-sidecar`, `checkpoint`, `unknown-role`, and `saved-final`. State what each means, name its rendered next action, and keep unknown saved metadata as unknown rather than explaining it as current truth.

- **Keep state actions bounded:** Explain that report-only, orphaned, malformed, checkpoint, and unknown-role evidence calls for inspecting the named artifact or obtaining a fresh audit when new evidence is needed. Explain that a saved final with `no-qualified-current-evidence` calls for a fresh audit because its saved result says no qualified current evidence exists; other saved finals point to their saved report and result, but do not establish that the result is current or comparable with another row. Do not tell operators to re-audit merely because more than one ledger row exists.

- **Clarify eligibility in the existing flow:** Update the `docs/how-to/pipeline/audit.md` report-artifact discussion and `### Pre-apply checkpoint vs. final report — the persisted role` reader boundary so visible ledger rows cannot be read as eligible history. `no-history` must mean no eligible ordinary-final convergence-history sidecar, not no file in the reports directory.

- **Cover Markdown-only Jira evidence:** Update `docs/how-to/pipeline/audit.md`’s `### Auditing against the jira-grade bundle (--grade jira)` explanation of the intentionally withheld JSON sidecar. A Jira-grade Markdown report is visible to the ledger as report-only evidence, but still does not enter convergence history, reuse, or a convergence verdict.

- **Keep the source map accurate:** In `docs/how-to/pipeline/audit.md:## Source map`, distinguish the existing role-aware history reader from the landed ledger reader and renderer. Cite the final step 2 symbols only after confirming them in the landed module.

- **Keep architecture at the layer boundary:** In `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:## Convergence diagnostic`, retain `load_report_history → diagnose_convergence → render_diagnosis` as the decision chain. Describe the ledger reader and renderer as a parallel, read-only completeness-artifact view that `plan_tool/cli/convergence_cmd.py:cmd_convergence` renders after the diagnosis.
