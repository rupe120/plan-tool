The Architecture section ("Open and pending") says "`open`, when the brief leaves the concern undecided and the row carries no choice, or chosen, when it carries a choice". Step 2 (`check_stack_totality`) reports only missing concern, unknown concern, and duplicate id. Step 1 leaves the row shape as a "Recommendation" ("exactly one of `choice` … or `open: true`") and says the parser enforces nothing about concerns. The Testing section lists no case for a row with both `choice` and `open`, or with neither. A row with both, or neither, would pass every gate. The first-run output, `INDEX.md`, and the review callout would then disagree about whether it is open. This reading rests on the detail excerpts shown, which were truncated.
  - Fix: Name the owner of the "exactly one of choice or open" rule (parser or stack gate). Add a violation kind for it to step 2 and a Testing case for both-set and neither-set rows.
- MEDIUM `[pending-decision-source]` [confidence: limited]: Plan and detail disagree on where pending decisions are read from, so the pending list can silently omit rows. The Architecture section ("Totality and version compatibility") says stack ids join the union "both stages resolve decision anchors against", so a decision in either the constraints or components stage can anchor to a stack row. Step 5 reads only "the head's `02-constraints.md` and its pending-decision source", and its Input says "domain and/or constraints artifact". Components is not mentioned. A pending decision in `03-components.md` anchored to a stack row would not appear in the pending list of the first-run output or `INDEX.md`. Step 6 has the same exposure. Step 6 describes the candidate's artifacts as four bytes, but it does not say which of them feed pending.
  - Fix: State once in the plan body which artifacts' decisions count toward a stack row's pending-ness (constraints and components). Reconcile steps 5 and 6 and the pending Testing cases to that.
- MEDIUM `[stack-product-compare-wording]` [confidence: limited]: Step 6's `## Why` still says the `product` values "are what a stack choice is judged against (for example, a stack choice naming a database next to a store row whose `product` differs)". The plan's Architecture ("One source of truth") says the summary "does not judge whether they differ: no comparison rule is defined". The plan's intent for step 6 says "The summary displays and does not compare." The plan body is the settled decision, so step 6's Why contradicts it. An implementer reading that Why could add a mismatch marker to the callout. That marker would be a comparison, and it would turn the display into a gate on stack content.
  - Fix: Reword step 6's Why so the `product` values appear beside the stack choice only for the reviewer to read. Remove "judged against" and the mismatch example, and state that the callout never flags a difference.
- MEDIUM `[stack-downgrade-absence]` [confidence: limited]: No site says what happens when a candidate's constraints artifact has no `stack` key but its predecessor had one. Architecture binds totality to "any artifact that carries the `stack` collection" or an explicit first-run signal. A candidate that drops the whole key therefore reads as "stack not recorded" and passes. That silently removes every recorded choice and every `open` concern. Step 6's candidate delta has nothing to show for it either, because the key is simply absent. This contradicts the Outcome that a stack choice "is never accepted unseen".
  - Fix: Pick one policy and state it in Architecture, step 2, and step 6. Either refuse a candidate that removes the collection when the predecessor carried it, or require the review callout to show "stack removed". Add the matching test.
