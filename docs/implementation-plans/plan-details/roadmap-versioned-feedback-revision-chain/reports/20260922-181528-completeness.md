```text
Architecture: “an awaiting-review item is stale when its bound candidate revision is no longer the current accepted head”

Acceptance criteria: “An unpromoted or rejected proposal remains reviewable in the ledger and is deterministically stale when its bound revision is not the accepted head.”
```
  - Fix: Define stale proposal status against whether the candidate remains promotable from its bound predecessor, and reconcile the ledger lifecycle, review, and promotion rules to that definition.
- HIGH `[candidate-generation-release]` [confidence: limited]: The one-candidate-per-head rule promises that later ledger edits “form a later batch only after the current generation is promoted or explicitly resolved,” but steps 5–10 never define who resolves an unpromoted or review-rejected generation, whether rejection performs that resolution, or how it releases the admission without changing its recorded proposals. Current `plan_tool/roadmap_candidate_run.py:run_initial_candidate_proposal` keeps returning the stored candidate while its admission records a candidate id, so the existing lifecycle supplies no release behavior. The plan therefore leaves reviewers unable to determine when a rejected candidate permits a later batch.

```text
Architecture: “One unpromoted candidate generation may exist per accepted head, but reviewer ledger edits remain allowed; they form a later batch only after the current generation is promoted or explicitly resolved.”
```

  - Fix: Specify the owner, result, and evidence of resolving a non-promoted candidate, including how a review rejection releases the next batch while preserving the prior candidate’s review record and ledger proposals.
