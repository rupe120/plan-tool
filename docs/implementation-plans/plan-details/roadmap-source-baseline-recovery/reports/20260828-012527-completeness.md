- HIGH `[baseline-lineage-currentness]` [confidence: limited]: `Architecture` says “active baseline remains the authority,” while steps 1–3, `Testing`, and acceptance criterion 3 never define recovery lineage. `CanonicalSourceIdentity` and `ROADMAP_FORMAT.md`’s “Versioned receipt reference” contain only binding fields and a digest, and `admitted_source_is_current`, `current_escalation_receipt`, and `current_impact_analysis` compare those identities for equality; returning a stage to a previously issued canonical source can therefore make old evidence current again.
  - Fix: Define immutable recovery lineage and a source-identity/currentness transition that keeps pre-recovery evidence non-current even if a later recovery restores the same digest.
- HIGH `[selected-range-admission]` [confidence: limited]: Step 1 and `Architecture` require a selected exact-canonical range plus a complete roadmap that passes current admission, but they never state that every unselected stage must still pass ordinary baseline-backed admission. This matters because `check_full_roadmap_gates` checks artifact gates while `capture_roadmap_impact_snapshot` separately admits every source: recovery can otherwise either bless gate-valid unselected drift or reject the selected recovery outright.
  - Fix: Define recovery-only admission for selected stages and require every unselected stage to retain ordinary baseline-backed admission before full-roadmap gates run.
- HIGH `[selected-range-atomicity]` [confidence: limited]: Step 1 (`Add a confirmed selected-range source-baseline recovery action`), `Architecture`, `Risks`, and acceptance criterion 2 treat the selected range as one safe action but do not define the result of interruption between baseline replacements. `enroll_stage_baselines` uses `_publish_generation` and `recover_roadmap_publication` to keep a recorded generation all old or all new; without the same recovery contract, a selected range can be left partially advanced.
  - Fix: Define selected-range recovery as one recoverable all-old-or-all-new baseline update, including restart and unrecorded-change refusal behavior.
- HIGH `[recovery-seed-state]` [confidence: limited]: Step 1 (`Add a confirmed selected-range source-baseline recovery action`), `Architecture`, `Testing`, and acceptance criterion 3 preserve artifacts and evidence but omit tracked seed state; `Out of scope` excludes seed materialization, not invalidation. `materialize_plan_seed()` reads every roadmap stage, while `roadmap_seed_state.py` can still mark derived seeds current after recovery changes constraints or components.
  - Fix: Define and test that recovery atomically invalidates affected tracked seed state without rewriting or materializing seed files.
- HIGH `[recovery-stale-input-recheck]` [confidence: limited]: Step 1 (`Add a confirmed selected-range source-baseline recovery action`), `Architecture`, `Risks`, `Testing`, and acceptance criterion 2 require validation before a trusted baseline changes but omit a final current-state check at the replacement boundary. Direct artifact edits are outside `roadmap_writer_guard()`, and `enroll_stage_baselines()` publishes baselines from its earlier reads, so a source can change after validation and leave a reported recovery bound to stale input.
  - Fix: Require recovery to re-read and revalidate the complete selected and unselected state at its baseline-replacement boundary, refusing with no changed baseline if any input moved.
