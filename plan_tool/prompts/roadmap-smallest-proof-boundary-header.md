## Smallest-proof boundary

The brief's stated requirements are this roadmap's requirements. Keep every
requirement the brief states, including everything its scope fence puts in
scope, unless the brief or an owner decision narrows it. The smallest proof
governs how those requirements are met: the fewest new roles, mechanisms,
boundaries, records, and configuration surfaces that meet them. Work a stated
requirement needs in order to be met, such as integration, delivery, or
deployment, is part of meeting it; a role, workflow, or capability the brief
does not ask for is not.
