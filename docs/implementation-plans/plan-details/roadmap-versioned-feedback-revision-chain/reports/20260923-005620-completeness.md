  ```text
  The candidate also stores a fresh, complete roadmap-derived feedback set for its own revision.   Derived-set replacement performs no matching, omission check, or warning.
  ```
  (Architecture)

- HIGH `[review-replacement-source-binding]` [confidence: limited]: Step 8 (`Bind candidate questions, answers, rejection, and discard resolution to the exact active-candidate batch`) assigns two incompatible source bindings to a sealed review replacement. Architecture says its `source_revision_id` is the reviewed candidate, while Scope guardrails, Testing, and acceptance criteria say it retains the reviewed candidate’s exact source; those are different whenever the reviewed candidate was itself created from an earlier root or active source. That leaves ancestry validation, batch identity, and promotion unable to agree on which source the replacement must prove.  
  ```text
  A sealed review replacement is also a successor: its immediate reviewed predecessor is its `source_revision_id`, while its retained original batch records the predecessor's original source separately.
  ```
  (Architecture)

  ```text
  A sealed review replacement atomically becomes the active candidate with the reviewed candidate's exact root, source, and batch evidence; its derived set is fresh for the replacement revision.
  ```
  (Acceptance criteria)
  - Fix: Define separate, consistently named bindings for the replacement’s immediate predecessor and its retained original batch source, then align the scope guardrails, lifecycle text, tests, and acceptance criteria to those bindings.
