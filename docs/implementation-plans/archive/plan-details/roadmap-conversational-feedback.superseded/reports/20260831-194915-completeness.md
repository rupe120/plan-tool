  - Fix: Define one content-bound manifest contract that fully authorizes the candidate footprint, binds its final identity to all semantic fields, and permits no later model interpretation or unlisted write after acceptance.
- HIGH `[extension-closure]` [confidence: limited]: Architecture > Closure reconciliation permits a flagged outside-closure semantic extension, while step 4 promises only to reconcile without shrinking the declared closure; neither requires deterministic closure reconciliation from each added node through to the final affected-stage set. `roadmap_impact_relations.resolve_impact_closure` closes only its original `ImpactTarget`, so an extension can omit a relation-connected row or artifact and still appear accepted.
  - Fix: Require every extension to expand through deterministic declared-relation closure until stable, and make manifest validation, candidate proof, and transaction membership use that verified union.
- HIGH `[final-snapshot-boundary]` [confidence: limited]: Risks > Snapshot staleness, Acceptance criteria, step 2 (`Publish one artifact and baseline per affected stage plus one seed-state member...`), and step 6 require stale proposals to leave canonical bytes unchanged, but do not require the final snapshot recheck to occur after this transaction’s complete journal is durable and before the first replacement. `roadmap_revision_publish._publish_generation` writes the journal before `before_replace`, while `recheck_roadmap_impact_snapshot` rejects a pending journal unless its exact generation is admitted with `allowed_pending_generation`; a normal post-journal recheck always refuses, while a pre-journal recheck leaves a change window.
  - Fix: Require final staleness validation at the journal boundary, admitting only this transaction’s complete generation and clearing that journal on refusal.
- HIGH `[manifest-acceptance-route]` [confidence: limited]: Step 7 (`Add exclusive --revise-cross-artifact and --accept-impact-manifest options, keep -y non-authoritative, and preserve the existing review-page atomic overwrite`) does not define the proposal command’s evidence input, the read-only review of the exact manifest, or the acceptance command’s required identity and conflict rules. `CROSS_ARTIFACT_WORKFLOW` is only a workflow string, and current `roadmap_cmd._render_impact_analysis_result` prints an identity but not proposal contents; the existing evidence views cover only escalations and impact analyses. Operators can therefore accept unseen content or implementations can bind a selector, receipt, analysis, and manifest inconsistently.
  - Fix: Define one exclusive proposal-and-review flow with exact evidence inputs, a redacted read-only manifest view, and acceptance of that same current manifest final.
- HIGH `[feedback-consumption-lifecycle]` [confidence: limited]: Step 5 records only a manifest lifecycle, while step 6, Architecture > Frozen contracts, Testing, and the acceptance criteria never state how a successful transaction consumes the selected open feedback row.
