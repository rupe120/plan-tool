- MEDIUM `[stack-totality-introducing-candidate]` [confidence: limited]: Totality has no rule for the candidate that first introduces the `stack` collection. Architecture ("Totality and version compatibility") says totality binds "on any candidate whose predecessor already carries the collection". Step 2 (stack gates) repeats this and adds the first-run signal. A candidate in a stack-less lineage carries no such predecessor, so it escapes totality. It can therefore introduce a partial stack, for example only a language/runtime row, or an empty `stack` list. The next candidate in that lineage then binds totality against that partial collection. Every unrelated feedback candidate is refused until someone adds the missing concerns. This contradicts the Risks entry and the acceptance criterion that unrelated candidates in a lineage are not refused over the collection. The Testing section's list of totality cases does not cover it.
  - Fix: State whether totality also binds on a candidate that itself carries the collection, regardless of its predecessor. Make step 2 implement that. Add a test for a candidate that introduces a partial stack and one that introduces an empty one.
- MEDIUM `[stack-pending-semantics]` [confidence: limited]: The plan describes "pending" in two incompatible ways. Architecture ("The `stack` collection") says "Stack rows can be targeted by feedback, and a choice may be `pending`", which reads as a state on the row's choice. Architecture ("Open and pending") says "Pending-ness is a fact about decisions: a decision anchored to a stack row's id with status `pending` makes that row pending". Step 1 (collection parsing) and step 3 (row-type maps and revision patch) could each add a `pending` value to the row schema. Step 5 (first-run and `INDEX.md` lists) and step 6 (candidate review) could then compute the pending list from different sources and disagree.
  - Fix: Delete "a choice may be `pending`" from the first Architecture paragraph. Say once that pending is derived only from decisions anchored to the row id, and that the row schema carries no pending value. Have steps 5 and 6 take the pending list from the one stack-summary producer.
