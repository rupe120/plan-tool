  `FeedbackRow.target` (`FeedbackTarget` in `plan_tool/roadmap_artifacts.py`) can point at the whole artifact (`FeedbackTargetKind.ARTIFACT`, no `row_id`) or at one specific row (`FeedbackTargetKind.ROW`, with `row_id` set).
