- HIGH `[detail-candidate-parse-failure]`: Step 5 (`Wire the concept sign-off gate into detail`) says “Malformed candidate output is fail-soft for that result,” while its Acceptance criteria require “Every detail-generation result” to emit everything routed to `## Deferred`. An unattended `scope-out --apply` can consequently convert an approved inclusion into an approved deferral without operator sign-off on that new state.
