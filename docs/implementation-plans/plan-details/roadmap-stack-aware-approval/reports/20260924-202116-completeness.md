
  - Fix: State once in the Stack route contract that a missing, non-candidate, or unvalidated hop makes the whole stack underivable and that steps 2 and 3 refuse it, separate from an absent stored route, which contributes nothing. Add that refusal to the Testing section and the acceptance criteria.
