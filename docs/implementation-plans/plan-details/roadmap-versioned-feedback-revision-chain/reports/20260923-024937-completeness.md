The former pointer remains bound to the old root after promotion, so the required root/active processing pair is invalid and the promoted roadmap cannot resume ordinary batching correctly.

```text
Architecture: “Promotion advances only the artifact head, clears the active-candidate pointer, and never rewrites reviewer feedback.”

Step 9 — Implementation: “Promotion must retain immutable version records, review records, active-candidate state, and reviewer-ledger bytes unchanged.”
```
  - Fix: Define promotion as one guarded accepted-head-and-active-pointer transition, while keeping the ledger and immutable candidate evidence unchanged, and align step 9’s implementation, tests, and criteria with that lifecycle.
- HIGH `[review-replacement-proposal-provenance]` [confidence: limited]: Step 8 (`Bind candidate questions, answers, rejection, and discard resolution to the exact active-candidate batch`) forbids mutating reviewer-owned input during sealed replacement, but Architecture requires that replacement carry the predecessor’s live awaiting-review items and rebind their live proposal provenance to the replacement. Without that ledger update, those proposals remain bound to the superseded candidate and become stale under Architecture’s current-proposal rule; Step 8’s replacement implementation, tests, and acceptance criteria do not deliver the required rebinding.

```text
Architecture: “A sealed review replacement carries its predecessor's live awaiting-review items and proposed responses into the replacement revision, rebinding their live proposal provenance to that replacement while retaining the predecessor's immutable evidence.”

Step 8 — Implementation: “Do not recheck live ledger eligibility or mutate reviewer-owned input during this path.”
```
  - Fix: Specify one guarded sealed-replacement transition that updates only the reviewed candidate’s live awaiting-review proposal provenance and response as required, preserves immutable predecessor evidence, and proves unrelated ledger items remain unchanged.

  - Fix: Establish the terminal pointer-release and immutable stale-resolution contract in step 5 for settlement to consume, or make step 7 depend on the step that owns that shared lifecycle contract.
