Its fields are `id`, `facet`, `value`, `product`, `description` and `research_disclosure`. Architecture says: "It includes every hosting row and every store row named by the plan row's components, each with category, sharing, and product." Step 1 says: "Every hosting row, and every store row named by the plan row's components, reaches the seed with its category, sharing, and product." An implementer cannot render a category or sharing for a hosting row. The test "the seed's rendered stack equals the accepted rows" has no defined target for hosting rows. The loss also differs: `facet` and `value` (for example `compute: managed-containers`) are the hosting row's content, and no step names them in the rendered fields.
  - Fix: State the rendered field set per row kind. Hosting rows carry id, facet, value and product, with the explicit open-product wording. Store rows carry id, category, sharing and product. Reconcile the Architecture section, step 1, and the "Verification" and "Case ownership" criteria to it.
- MEDIUM `[promoted-seed-stack-source]` [confidence: limited]: The plan contradicts itself on whether promoted seeds carry the stack. Step 1's title says "the draft and promoted-seed overwrites and the sidecar rewrite stay as they are, now writing the stack-bearing text", so promoted-seed overwrites write stack text. Step 2 says "A seed with no stack section, such as an older draft or a promoted seed, carries nothing". The Risks section says "promoted seeds and plans already derived from them … keep their current text". Whether a promoted seed rematerialized after this change has the stack decides if `create` reads a stack for it or states "no stack section". The criterion "A seed with no stack section carries nothing and the plan says so" depends on that choice.
  - Fix: Decide whether a promoted seed written after this change carries the stack section. Then align step 1's title, step 2's "such as … a promoted seed" example, and the Risks entry to that decision. For example, say that only promoted seeds written before this change lack the section.
