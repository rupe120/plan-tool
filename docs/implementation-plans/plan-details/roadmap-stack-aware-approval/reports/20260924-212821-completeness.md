- MEDIUM `[hop-kinds-no-route-hops]` [confidence: limited]: The Architecture "Hop kinds" table leaves two kinds of no-route hop unplaced, and steps 1, 4, and 6 and acceptance criterion 4 all read from it. Two implementers can print or read these hops differently.
  - (a) The table has no row for a pre-route batch candidate. Row 3 groups it with selector-era candidates and gives it "no view input":

  ```
  | selector-era or route-less candidate (no recorded root) | contributes nothing | unchanged: a live legacy contract with no view input keeps its current output |
  ```
  (Architecture, Hop kinds, row 3.)

  At HEAD these are two populations. Selector-era evidence has no `root_version_id`. Pre-route batch evidence has a recorded root and a batch receipt but no `normalized_operations` or `operation_classification`. `roadmap_candidate_run.py:_require_reproducible_batch_operations` says "Existing batch candidates and replacements predate route evidence", and `CandidateProposalEvidence` allows both route fields to be `None` beside a batch receipt. A pre-route batch candidate fits neither row 1 ("batch-lineage candidate (stored route)") nor row 3 ("no recorded root"). For a multi-hop stack of such candidates, the view can be built, so it is unclear whether the printout applies or the output stays unchanged.
  - (b) Row 2 does not say which record supplies a sealed answered replacement's hop fields:

  ```
  | sealed answered replacement (its own record, which stores no route) | contributes nothing | on the answered route, review and answer list the reviewed candidate as the top hop and promote lists the replacement in its place, with identical hops beneath; a field its record does not store is labeled absent, never invented |
  ```
  (Architecture, Hop kinds, row 2.)

  At HEAD, `roadmap_candidate_review.py:_store_batch_replacement` writes two records for a batch replacement. One is `CandidateReviewReplacement`. The other is a `CandidateProposalEvidence` at `candidate_evidence_path`, so `load_stored_candidate_proposal` loads it. Its batch receipt is the reviewed candidate's own, so `reviewer_items` are stored and only the route is missing. "Its own record" can mean either file. One implementer prints the receipt's selected feedback and another labels it "absent". When a replacement is a middle hop under a later candidate, the digest content differs too.
  - Fix: Give each no-route hop kind its own row: selector-era with no recorded root, pre-route batch with a recorded root, and sealed answered replacement. For each row, name the record the hop's fields are read from, what prints for route and selected feedback, and whether the stack printout applies. Then align steps 1, 4, and 6 and acceptance criterion 4 to those rows.
- MEDIUM `[stack-printout-route-conditional-lines]` [confidence: limited]: Step 4 (`Build the whole-stack view, its digest, and its one shared printout`) carries the current disclosure's fixed `promotion effects` and `choices` lines onto every surface, so on a multi-hop stack with no structural hop both lines would be false. The Architecture "Stack view" bullet and step 4 make the top hop keep the whole disclosure and add "the promotion effects", with no condition on the stack's route:

  ```
  The top hop keeps everything the current structural disclosure prints (typed operations, batch receipt identity, non-goals, labeled empty categories, and the choices line), so the view extends that disclosure and never replaces it.
  ```
  (Architecture, "Stack view".)

  The Hop kinds table prints the view for any batch-lineage stack of more than one hop, and step 6 renders it on the proposal output and on the answered route's review, answer, and promote-review-seal output. At HEAD, `_render_structural_candidate_disclosure` in `plan_tool/cli/roadmap_cmd.py` prints `promotion effects: accepted head advances; active candidate releases; enrolled seed state invalidates` and `choices: approve, edit, reject` unconditionally, because it runs only for a structural candidate. A stack with no structural hop promotes with no seed invalidation, and `--decide-candidate` refuses it (`_require_structural_candidate`). So the shared printout would tell the operator that seed state invalidates and offer choices the command refuses. A reader who drops both lines from the shared printout instead removes the seed-invalidation disclosure the structural decision shows today. Both readings satisfy step 4 and acceptance criterion 4 as written, and they differ on a fact the operator relies on when approving. Affected: Architecture "Stack view"; step 4; step 6; the Hop kinds "Stack printout" column; acceptance criterion 4. The plan body owns the condition; detail owns wording and layout.
  - Fix: State once in the Stack view bullet that the promotion-effects line and the choices line follow the stack's route, not the surface: a structural stack states the seed-state invalidation and offers approve, edit, and reject, while a stack with no structural hop states no seed invalidation and offers no `--decide-candidate` choices. Then align step 4, step 6, the Hop kinds printout column, and criterion 4 to it, with one test case for a multi-hop stack with no structural hop.

- MEDIUM `[digest-coverage-obligation]` [confidence: limited]: Step 4 (`Build the whole-stack view, its digest, and its one shared printout`) promises a digest that covers the view's content and never depends on the printout, and Risks names both failure modes. No acceptance criterion or Testing entry obliges either property:

  ```
  A digest that includes rendering can refuse a valid confirm. A digest that leaves out a hop can confirm a stack the operator never saw.
  ```
  (Risks, last bullet.)

  Every confirm/refuse row in Testing compares a printed digest with one recomputed by the same function. A digest that drops a hop, or one that changes when only the printout's wording changes, passes all of those rows. The two Risks entries can therefore come true with a green suite. Criterion 3 ("seals and promotes only when the supplied digest equals the digest recomputed for the still-active candidate") pins matching, not coverage or independence from rendering.
  - Fix: Add one acceptance criterion and one Testing entry. The digest must change when any single hop's stored content changes or the hop set changes, and it must not change when only the printout's wording or layout changes.
- MEDIUM `[deliberate-omissions-unpinned]` [confidence: limited]: Testing and the acceptance criteria assert the output carve-outs for selector-era candidates and single-hop presentation-only proposals, but not three other exclusions the plan states on purpose. A change that adds the stack or a confirm step to these surfaces keeps every listed test green.
  - Step 6 (`Render step 4's shared stack printout on the proposal output ...`) says "standalone --reject-candidate and --discard-candidate output is unchanged". Scope guardrails says the same exclusion is Not allowed:

  ```
  Stack output on `--reject-candidate` or `--discard-candidate`. They neither ask for nor confirm an approval and keep their contracts; only the structural decision's reject carries the note.
  ```
  (Scope guardrails, Not allowed.)
  - Step 6 also has the structural decision's reject output carry the "reject drops every unpromoted hop" note. No criterion locks that behavior in.
  - Scope guardrails refuses "A confirm step for edit or reject, including rejecting a multi-hop stack". Step 5 (`Split structural approve into two invocations`) confirms only approve. Nothing pins that edit and reject still run with no confirm flag.

  Testing's carve-out list ("each asserted as a carve-out") names only the legacy-route, all-presentation, selector-era, and single-hop cases.
  - Fix: Add the three exclusions to Testing's carve-out list and to acceptance criterion 4 or a new criterion. Standalone reject and discard output stays exactly as before. The structural decision's reject output carries the note. Edit and reject run with no confirm flag.
- MEDIUM `[own-route-site-inventory]` [confidence: limited]: The Guard sites table says it lists "Every own-route site the sweep changes or keeps, with its owner", but it omits the own-route check in `cli/roadmap_cmd.py:_render_candidate_proposal_result`. That check, `operation_classification.value == "structural"`, decides whether the disclosure prints on the proposal output. Left as is, a presentation-only C2 over a structural C1 prints only its own diff, which is the top-hop-only defect. The Problem statement's own list of own-route guards omits it too. Step 6 covers the proposal output, so the fix is implied, but the table is the inventory Risks tells implementers to enumerate from, and it names no owner for this site.
  - Fix: Add a row to Guard sites for the proposal-output own-route check with step 6 as its owner, and add that site to the Problem statement's list of own-route checks.
