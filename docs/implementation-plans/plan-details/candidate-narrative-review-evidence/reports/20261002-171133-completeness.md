- MEDIUM `[removed-id-source-and-pruning]` [confidence: limited]: Step 2 (`removed-id check`) and the Architecture section say removed ids come from "the `removed_id` records carried by the predecessor artifact's `artifact.removals` plus any the candidate adds", and that "a record the reviewer pruned is deliberately no longer checked". These two rules conflict. If the source is the predecessor's records, a prune made in the candidate has no effect, because the predecessor still carries the record. If the source is the candidate's own carried records, the predecessor is not the source. The plan never says which artifact's pruning counts, or at which version the prune takes effect. Step 1 owns the rule, and steps 2 and 3 (Input) and the Testing case "a pruned removal record is no longer checked" all depend on it. Two implementers could build opposite behavior: a prune that bypasses the check, or one that does not.
  - Fix: State once in step 1's section, and mirror it in the Architecture section and the step 2 and 3 inputs, exactly which artifact's removal records form the checked set. Say how a prune in the candidate relative to its predecessor is detected, and which Testing case pins each outcome.
- MEDIUM `[pure-function-predecessor-input]` [confidence: limited]: Step 3's title says the evidence is computed "from the candidate alone, with no model call and no storage", and its summary says "no filesystem access". Step 3's Input also says the check's removed-id source "depends on the predecessor". It tells the implementer to "read at HEAD how the candidate paths already load the predecessor", which means a filesystem read. Step 4's helper then takes `plans_dir`/`slug` "if step 3's adapter needs them to load the predecessor". The plan does not say whether the predecessor is part of the candidate input (so the function stays pure) or is loaded inside the adapter (so it does not). This affects whether a stored candidate and its in-memory form give identical evidence, and where a missing or unreadable predecessor surfaces: as a refusal, or as an empty evidence list that approves silently.
  - Fix: Decide in step 3 whether the predecessor is an explicit input to the pure function or is resolved by the adapter. Define the behavior when the predecessor cannot be loaded, then align steps 4 and 5 to that decision.
