- HIGH `[reviewer-visible-candidate-comparison]` [confidence: limited]: The Problem statement requires promotion of “exactly what the reviewer saw,” but step 7 (`Generate diff-anchored questions…`) sends the deterministic diff only to the model and treats question text as opaque, while step 11 (`Expose enrollment, candidate review…`) prints only the question and anchor and says “do not print candidate artifact contents, reconstruct a diff.” Step 9 (`Route roadmap views through accepted head…`) can show only accepted content, not an inert candidate or its replacement, leaving no reviewer-visible comparison before answer or promotion.
  - Fix: provide a bounded read-only handoff of each stored candidate’s exact identity and persisted predecessor diff before its answer or seal can be promoted, and prove the final displayed candidate is the sealed one.
- HIGH `[legacy-writer-fence]` [confidence: limited]: Step 10 (`Refuse enrolled roadmaps before legacy artifact, baseline, seed, or journal overwrites and deletes while preserving unversioned writes`) promises to “Refuse every legacy roadmap mutation path,” but its direct-entry-point list and tests omit seed materialization.   - Fix: apply the enrolled-roadmap refusal to every seed-materialization entry and its CLI route before model invocation or seed/report writes, with enrolled and unversioned coverage.
